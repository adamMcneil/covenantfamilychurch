The [Winton Family](http://thewintonsbluegrass.com/) will be with us for a FREE concert on Friday, May 20th (7-8:30 PM) at St. Stephen United Methodist Church in Troy, MO. Come prepared to buy a few of thier CD’s and to give an offering to help with thier expenses.

Randy, Cody, and Jesse Winton have been playing bluegrass music together for the past four years–*and they are really talented*. They will certainly give us some foot-stomping, hand-clapping, God-glorifying music! Feel free to invite your friends and relatives. The concert begins at 7:00 PM on Friday night.
