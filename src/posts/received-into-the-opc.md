[<img src="/wp-content/uploads/2012/11/OPC-Logo-e1430954491111.png" alt="OPC Logo" width="100" height="136" class="alignleft" loading="lazy">](/wp-content/uploads/2012/11/OPC-Logo-e1430954491111.png)This past Sunday evening, May 3, we were formally received into the Midwest Presbytery of the Orthodox Presbyterian Church. I was installed as the pastor of the church, and several members of the Midwest Presbytery and two nearby OPC congregations (Christ OPC in St. Charles, MO and Gateway OPC in Town and Country, MO) joined us for the special service and celebration.

This service was the culmination of a year-long odyssey of transferring from one denomination to another. God has cared for us, protected us, and shepherded us through the entire process. We believe that the OPC will be a healthy and prosperous denominational home for us. As we receive much from them, we also hope to give much to them in return. May God be praised!

— Pastor Marcus Serven
