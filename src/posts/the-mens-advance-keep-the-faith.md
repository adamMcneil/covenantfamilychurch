**The Men’s Advance** is an event hosted by Covenant Family Church as an opportunity for men and young men to gather and learn to be mature Christians and godly leaders of their households. On August 21st we will be gathering to spur one another onward, to run the race with endurance and to contend for the faith once delivered to the saints.

**To register with 50% discount**, send an email to Pastor Bringe [here](mailto:peterjb@truevine.net). Registration is normally, $60 per person with a $240 max per family, so with the discount it will be $30 per person with a $120 max per family.

This year’s speakers are Christian McShaffrey and Peter Bringe. The event will be held Saturday, August 21st, at the Family Vision Library (2020 Parkway Dr, St Peters, MO 63376). **Learn more** about the event [at this link](/mens-advance/).
