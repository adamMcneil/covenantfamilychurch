The 2014 Men’s Advance is coming!

**—Dates:** Friday, February 28 (begins with dinner) – Saturday, March 1, 2014 (all day)

**—Our Theme:** “Manly Courage” based on Joshua 1:9 – “Be strong and courageous. Do not be frightened, and do not be dismayed, for the LORD your God is with you wherever you go.”

**—Our Speakers:** Scott Brown and Marcus Serven

**—Our Musician:** Charlie Zahm will give a FREE concert for the entire family on Saturday evening, March 1 (7:00-8:30 PM)

—**Our Location:** at the facilities of St. Stephen United Methodist Church, in Troy, MO

There will be more information coming–on how to register, conferences costs, schedule, etc.

I hope to see you in late February!

–Pastor Marcus J. Serven
