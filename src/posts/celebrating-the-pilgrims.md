Please join us on Sunday, November 20th as we worship God and remember His providential care for the English Pilgrims. Following our afternoon worship service (1-3:15 PM) we will enjoy a wonderful Thanksgiving feast (3:30-5:15 PM). After that we will gather for an informal Thanksgiving Service (5:30-7:00 PM) where we’ll sing some of the great hymns of the faith and hear some brief talks on the Pilgrims given by Peter Bringe, Dan Ford, and Pastor Serven.

Let us rejoice in the goodness of God for preserving his people through many trials and tribulations. Think of God’s promise to His people when you recall the Pilgrims: “He provides food for those who fear him; he remembers his covenant forever.” (Psalm 111:5)
