Mr. Charlie Zahm will entertain us with Scottish ballads, Early American folksongs, and some of his latest musical compositions.

<img src="/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/Charlie-Zahm-1.jpeg" alt="Charlie Zahm #1" width="183" height="275" class="alignright" loading="lazy">

**Who is Charlie Zahm?** Charlie is one of the most popular solo artists at Celtic music festivals, Maritime and Early American music events anywhere east of the Mississippi *(and for that matter, West of the Mississippi too)*. With his rich baritone voice and masterful guitar playing, Charlie weaves moments of history for the listener and viewer alike, with passion for the performance and a chosen repertoire pleasing to all members of the family.

The concert will be held at the facilities of St. Stephen United Methodist Church (located at 101 St. Stephen lane, Troy, MO 63379). An offering will be taken to help defer expenses in bringing Charlie to Missouri. Also, CD’s of Charlie’s music will be available for purchase. All are welcome to attend.

— Pastor Marcus J. Serven
