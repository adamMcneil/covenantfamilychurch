What are you seeking?

Do you seek love and relationships in the midst of a commercialized world?

Do you seek enduring truth and a multi-generational vision in the midst of fads and fleeting fashions?

Do you desire the peace that flows from forgiveness and belonging?

So do we.

These things are not found by positive thinking or mere vision casting. Rather, their only true and lasting source is Jesus Christ.

He brings us into a relationship with our Creator, a relationship of steadfast love known as a covenant. This covenant also binds us together as a [covenant family](/vision/).

In the bonds of this covenant we find God’s love, truth, forgiveness, and home. Come [join us this Sunday](/sunday/) as we renew this covenant in worship.

For more information on what to expect when you come [click here](/what-can-i-expect-on-sunday/).
