Come join us for the **2021 Pilgrim Heritage Celebration**! This year in particular holds special significance as the 400th anniversary of the first thanksgiving. We will remember the Pilgrims and give thanks to God with a program which will begin at 2:00 pm. You can join us then or you can come earlier for our potluck lunch around 1:00 pm, bringing food to share. You are also welcome to join us for our regular worship service at 11:00 am.

This year’s speakers will be Jeff Hamann, Dan Ford, and our pastor, Peter Bringe. Dan Ford is the author of *The Legacy Of Liberty and Property* and *In the Name of God, Amen: Rediscovering Biblical and Historical Covenants*.

Date: Saturday, November 21, 2021\
Location: Progress Park Banquet Center (968 Meyer Road, Wentzville 63385)\
Speakers: Rev. Peter Bringe, Jeff Hamann, and historian Dan Ford.
