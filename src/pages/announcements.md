**Blog Post**

[What Is the Lord’s Supper?](https://www.forchristskingdom.com/2026/11/what-is-lords-supper.html)  
In this installment on the blog, I explain question 96 of the Shorter Catechism, which asks, “What is the Lord’s Supper?” It answers, “The Lord’s supper is a sacrament, wherein, by giving and receiving bread and wine according to Christ’s appointment, his death is showed forth; and the worthy receivers are, not after a corporal and carnal manner, but by faith, made partakers of his body and blood, with all his benefits, to their spiritual nourishment and growth in grace.”

**Pilgrim Heritage Celebration**

Our annual Pilgrim Heritage Celebration will take place on the afternoon of Sunday, November 22nd. The speakers will be myself, Jeff Hamann, and E. Wesley Reynolds. Since this year is the 250th anniversary of American independence (1776), some of this year’s talks will connect the heritage of the Pilgrims and our country’s founding. I plan to speak about the reasons the Pilgrims had for establishing their colony, Jeff Hamann plans to speak about the founding fathers as dutiful sons, and Wesley Reynolds plans to speak about days of thanksgiving in the founding era.
