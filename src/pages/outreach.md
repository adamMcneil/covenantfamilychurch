We are committed to reaching out to all the members of our community with the message of God’s Word.

Therefore, don’t be surprised if you meet one of members and they end up inviting you to church.

Here are some examples of our regular outreach efforts:

WORSHIP – We invite the public to [join us for worship](/sunday/) each week as we seek God’s face and hear his word.

DISCIPLESHIP – In addition to our worship services, we also have a time for instruction on Sunday mornings at 10:00 am and a mid-week Bible Study every Wednesday evening for those who seek to grow in grace and knowledge.

EVENTS – We seek to be involved in various community events, providing a gospel witness. We also host some of our own events, such as a regular Thanksgiving event focusing on the godly legacy of the Pilgrims. In December we go Christmas caroling together, singing of the good news of Christ’s birth.

MERCY – Our Deacons minister to the needs of the poor, first within our own church family, and then to those outside (i.e., as resources allow).
