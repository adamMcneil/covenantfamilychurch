We gather together in person for worship and encourage you to [join us](/sunday/). But for those unable to make it, we livestream our services as well.

The livestream will appear on our YouTube channel, which you can find [at this link](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCgxGIBIWG2Z6cc4Iv2dxwoA). Our Sunday school lesson begins at 10am. Our worship service begins at 11am. Our evening service begins at 7pm.

[Contact us](/contact/) ahead of time if you want us to send you the bulletin. The sermons and lessons are also recorded and available on our [sermon page](/sermons/). The videos remain available afterwards on our YouTube channel, and if you have difficulty finding them after they are finished, look for them [here](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCgxGIBIWG2Z6cc4Iv2dxwoA/playlists). You can also check our [announcements page](/announcements/) for more information.
